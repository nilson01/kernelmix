# Changelog

## 0.2.0

- Poisson and NB2 count models with log observation-exposure offsets.
- Right-censored proportional hazards with a piecewise-exponential baseline.
- Gaussian random intercepts, known/new-cluster prediction and intercept summaries.
- Survival/hazard predictions and plots; independent likelihood and posterior checks.

## 0.1.0

- Gaussian and probit BKMR with component or grouped exposure selection.
- Joint predictions, exposure contrasts, diagnostics and non-pickle persistence.
- Response, surface, inclusion and trace plots.
- Fixed-state R comparisons and small independently integrated posterior checks.
